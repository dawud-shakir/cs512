Robot Arm with Scene-Graph Lighting

A jointed arm in a lit room. The arm, the room, the gear and all four lights are nodes of one scene graph, so each light moves with whatever it hangs from. Picking up the gear reparents it under the gripper; dropping it reparents it back to the room, where it falls and settles flat.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, arrow keys and w/s to move the camera. The sliders pose the joints, the buttons under the canvas run scripted motions, and the shaders on the left are live.

Pick-up uses two-link planar inverse kinematics: the base turns to face the gear, the elbow angle comes from the law of cosines for the wrist's distance from the shoulder, the shoulder angle is the target direction minus the elbow's offset, and the wrist cancels both so the gripper points straight down into the hole.
