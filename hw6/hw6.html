Baby Gen

Scene-graph lighting and texture mapping:
The floor, walls, ivory arm segments, playmat and sign use normal mapping with an aligned texture;
the cone, sun, cubes and the dark arm parts are bump-mapped from their textures;

Edit the shaders on the left; the canvas updates as you type.
Drag with the mouse to rotate the model.
Use the arrow keys and w, s keys to move the camera. Scroll to zoom.
Use the slider and dropdown to rotate about an axis; the button starts/stops spinning.
